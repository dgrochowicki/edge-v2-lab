## Post-match evaluation

The bet lost because Eternal Fire won both opening maps and the expected split-map structure did not materialize.

The key failure was not the Dust2 leg of the thesis. Eternal Fire winning its own pick was fully compatible with the pre-match model.

The decisive failure was Mirage: K27 did not convert the map that formed the main counterweight to the Eternal Fire Dust2 edge.

This matters for the final experiment review because Bet #7 was a relatively clean test of the recurring Over 2.5 framework:

- veto was known
- each side had an explicit map-winning path
- probability, fair-odds range and break-even were frozen before entry
- no live chase or additional exposure was added
- nevertheless, the split-map thesis failed and the series ended 2–0

This result is evidence against treating a simple “one strong map each” structure as sufficient on its own to establish betting edge.

No methodology rule is rewritten mid-experiment. The observation should be carried into the final 10-bet review.
