## Post-match evaluation

The 0–2 result does not by itself prove that the BET was bad.

However, the post-match audit identified a material pre-match process failure independent of the outcome:

> The analysis treated Bushido Wildcats as effectively having the stronger / more stable roster situation, but the match page listed coach yT as a substitute for Muk0s.

This means a critical roster input was not correctly verified before the market was promoted to CANDIDATE/BET.

Because roster verification is part of the EDGE v2 evidence verification gate, the final 64–67% probability estimate cannot be considered sufficiently supported by the frozen evidence.

This is an **analysis correction**, not a conclusion created from the losing result.

The appropriate process response would have been:

- stop the promotion to BET
- reassess the effect of the Bushido stand-in
- rebuild the roster and map thesis
- only then calculate a new probability and compare it with STS 1.70

No replacement probability should be invented after the result.
