## Adversarial check / main risks

The BET was not based on treating Leo's raw 89% map win rate as an 89% true probability.

Important counter-evidence and uncertainty included:

- Leo had already played a full BO3 earlier the same day, creating a real fatigue / double-header risk
- Leo had lost Nuke 10–13 to ex-RUSTEC the previous day
- part of Leo's Nuke sample came against lower-tier opposition
- Spirit Academy's nine-map Nuke sample was relatively small
- Spirit Academy had shown it could win Nuke, including a recent overtime win over Azuolas
- the overall evidence quality remained MEDIUM rather than HIGH

After these discounts, the working range was kept at `63–69%`.

## Post-match evaluation

The winning result is consistent with the original map-specific thesis, but the result alone does not validate the 63–69% probability estimate.

What was good about the process:

- the market was selected independently and specifically
- the veto created a concrete map matchup rather than a generic team-strength argument
- the thesis identified why Nuke was the relevant market
- the raw win-rate gap was explicitly discounted rather than copied directly into the probability estimate
- fatigue, opponent quality and the recent Nuke loss were considered before BET

What remains uncertain:

- the true opponent-adjusted strength of Leo's 19-map Nuke sample
- how much same-day fatigue should reduce a map probability
- whether the estimated 63–69% range was still too optimistic

No methodology rule should be changed because this bet won.
