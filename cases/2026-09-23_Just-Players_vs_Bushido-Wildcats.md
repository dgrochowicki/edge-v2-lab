## Adversarial check at decision time

### Why the market could be wrong

- Bushido carried the stronger baseline ranking
- Just Players had two substitutes
- Bushido appeared to have a credible route through Cache and the decider
- the final STS price still required only 58.8% break-even
- the confirmed maps were interpreted as supporting a series probability above that threshold

### Why we could be wrong

- Just Players remained dangerous despite the substitutes
- online BO3 variance was meaningful
- Mirage gave Just Players a credible map-winning path
- map win rates were not perfectly opponent-adjusted
- the evidence quality was only MEDIUM
- the roster comparison had not been verified correctly on both sides

The last point became the material analysis correction after audit.

---

## Material analysis correction

The pre-match reasoning treated Bushido Wildcats as having a roster-stability advantage and effectively as the more complete lineup.

That input was wrong.

The match information listed:

`yT (coach) substitutes Muk0s`

Therefore Bushido also entered the series with a material roster change.

This information existed before the match and should have been captured by the EDGE v2 evidence verification gate.

This is not hindsight from the 0–2 result.

It is a pre-match data-verification failure discovered during the audit.

### Why this matters

The Bushido ML thesis materially relied on the contrast:

`Bushido roster stability vs Just Players substitutes`

Once Bushido's own coach stand-in is included, that mechanism becomes substantially weaker and must be rebuilt rather than carried forward unchanged.

The 64–67% estimate was therefore produced from an incorrectly verified critical input.

The correct EDGE v2 process should have been:

1. keep or return the match to `WATCH`
2. assess the yT-for-Muk0s substitution
3. re-check whether historical Bushido map samples were representative of the actual lineup
4. rebuild the map-specific ML thesis
5. only then publish a new probability
6. compare that new estimate with STS 1.70

No corrected probability is assigned after the result because doing so would create hindsight precision.

## Post-match observation

Two things must be kept separate.

### Outcome

Bushido lost 0–2, including losing its own Cache pick.

That alone is not enough to label the original bet process wrong.

### Process

The roster verification failure is enough to say that the final BET did not fully satisfy EDGE v2's own evidence gate.

The important lesson is not:

> Do not bet teams with stand-ins.

The important lesson is:

> A roster-stability thesis cannot pass the evidence gate until the roster status of both teams has been explicitly verified.

This is a case-level observation for later review.

No global methodology rule is changed from this single match.
