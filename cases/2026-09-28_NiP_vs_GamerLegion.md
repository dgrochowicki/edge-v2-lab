## Market-specific hypotheses and adversarial check
- **NiP ML:** Nuke is NiP's clearest strength and GL's small-sample Mirage figure suggests another possible NiP path. Against this: GL's Cache record contrasts sharply with NiP's, Ancient is close, and NiP's Dust2 is weak. Claiming NiP has a clearly broader BO5 pool was an **analysis error**, corrected after reviewing the comparative map table. The screenshot also shows limitations for GL, but these alone do not establish NiP > break-even.
- **NiP +0.5 maps:** needs at least one NiP map; plausible through Nuke, but vulnerable to actual veto and cannot be assumed nearly certain. NiP +0.5 is distinct from +1.5.
- **Over 4.5 maps:** requires 3:2 either way. Mixed map strengths alone do not substantiate a probability exceeding price threshold.
- **Handicaps / Over 3.5:** no independently verified, market-specific mispricing thesis.

### Why might the market be wrong?
A BO5 may expose GL's weaker or untested maps; NiP has a strong Nuke sample and comparatively stronger Mirage results.

### Why might we be wrong?
GL's Cache is strong in a small sample, NiP has several poor map records, the opponent quality and sample comparability are unverified, veto can remove NiP's clearest route, and the bookmaker may already account for these facts. GL's recent 2:0 over magic is relevant context but does not prove final-series strength.

**Evidence quality: MEDIUM for descriptive map records, insufficient for a defensible precise series probability.** No fabricated `p`, fair odds or edge estimate.
