The over thesis depended on a split-map mechanism:

- Eternal Fire had the stronger path on Dust2

## Experiment interpretation

This is an important negative case for the current Over 2.5 framework.

Unlike some earlier losses, Bet #7 had:

- confirmed veto
- explicit map-specific paths
- a frozen probability range
- a frozen fair-odds range
- a clear adversarial sweep scenario

The market still won decisively through the exact principal counter-case: Eternal Fire took both opening maps.

The result does not by itself prove that the probability estimate was wrong, but across the growing sample it increases concern that the recurring “one map each” heuristic may be too weak or too optimistic when converted into a 57–60% Over 2.5 probability.

Do not modify the method mid-test. Preserve this as evidence for the final 10-bet evaluation.
